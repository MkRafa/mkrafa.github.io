02 / Approach

How I work.

A PM who prototypes. I get close enough to the models to know where they break, then design the product around it.

i.

Prioritise by value vs effort

Write the PRD and define the solution architecture for every use case before a sprint starts.

ii.

Prototype before handoff

I build agent flows in Claude and Lovable to stress-test edge cases, so AI scientists get a concrete spec.

iii.

LLMs only where judgment is needed

Everything else stays deterministic, which keeps latency, cost and auditability under control.

iv.

Tie AI to business KPIs

CSAT, AHT, retention, adoption. Every feature is reviewed with customers against a number.

03 / Builds

Hands-on builds.

I prototype to pressure-test product ideas before they reach a roadmap. These are where I work out what "good" looks like and how to measure it.

Healthcare · Agentic RAG

Coverage Determination Agent

Agentic RAGMCP152-case evals
Problem
Coverage questions ("is procedure C covered for condition D under plan L on date T?") need an answer and the policy clause that proves it.
Approach
An agent retrieves and cites policy clauses, a verifier re-checks every citation, and a rules-based gate decides whether to answer, escalate to a human, or refuse.
Principle
The models reason, but they never make the final call.
